WUREC’s Mission

The Wharton Undergraduate Real Estate Club is dedicated to exposing Penn undergrads to the real estate industry and its various opportunities. It seeks to provide an intellectual environment that not only allows students to learn, but also to network and build meaningful foundations for success in real estate.

WUREC provides increased access to many Zell/Lurie Real Estate Center offerings, including the Ballard Executive Visitor Luncheon Series, the Career Mentor Program, and the Spring and Fall Members’ Meetings. Additionally, active members will also have access to MBA/Undergraduate Real Estate Club Mixers, site visits, MBA Lecture Series events, and the Resume Book.

Whether you want to learn about investing and job opportunities, or simply have a general interest in real estate, please sign up via the link below. Then, look out for upcoming speakers, social events, and opportunities!

All members must attend at least three events per semester to retain membership.

What We Do

Treks

Small group trips to top real estate firms and property sites throughout the year. Recent Trek highlights have included NYC, Philadelphia, Dubai and Chicago.

Speakers

In-person speaker series and panels that expose students to real estate insights from industry leaders. Past speakers have come from Blackstone, Goldman Sachs, and PJT, as well as real estate CEOs.

Case Comps

WUREC teams compete in real estate case competitions across the country, including a 1st place, $10K finish at the International Cornell ICSC Retail Real Estate Case Competition.
