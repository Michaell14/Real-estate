Speakers

Conversations with the people who run the industry.

Practical, real-world perspective from leaders in real estate, finance, and investing.

The Speakers Committee hosts in-person speaker series and panels designed to expose university students to real estate insights from industry leaders. In the past, we've hosted speakers from Blackstone, Goldman Sachs, and PJT as well as real estate CEOs including Ben Ashkenazy and Charles Kushner.
