Treks

Inside the firms and the buildings.

Small group trips to top real estate firms and property sites throughout the year.

On our treks, WUREC members have conversations with real estate industry leaders and tour major properties. Some recent Trek highlights have included NYC, Los Angeles, Houston, Boston, Chicago, Dubai and London. Trek applications are open to all members of WUREC.
