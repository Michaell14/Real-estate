NYC 11.2021

New York City Trek · November 2021

During the November 2021 New York City trek, WUREC members visited the top floor of 3 World Trade Center to meet executives from Silverstein Properties, took a tour of Manhattan West with Brookfield's Development Team, and met with executives from Vornado Realty Trust about their transformative PENN 1 office development.
