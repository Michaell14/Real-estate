The WUREC team beat 26 teams, won 1st place, and took home the $10K prize.

Head of WUREC's Membership & Development Committee, Katherine Salvatori, teamed up with Sander Depietri and Amrajan Aujla to compete at the 2017 International Cornell ICSC Retail Real Estate Case Competition.
